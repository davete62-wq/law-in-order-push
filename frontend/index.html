Who we are

More than a debate club.

Law in Order is a student-led club at KB Academy for students who want to think harder, speak clearer, and understand the world more honestly.

⚖

Our purpose

We exist because law isn't just for lawyers. Understanding rights, justice and how decisions are made matters for everyone — especially for young people finding their voice.

✳

Our approach

We learn by doing: debating, examining real cases, researching legal history, and listening respectfully to opposing views. No grandstanding — just honest inquiry.

↗

Our community

A place to build confidence, sharpen your research skills, and develop the courage to express an informed opinion — even when it's uncomfortable.

Inside the club

Ideas in action.

Every activity is a chance to question, research, collaborate and grow.

01

Structured debates

Build arguments, support claims with evidence and respond to challenging questions under real debate conditions.

02

Case analysis

Study landmark and contemporary legal cases, identify key issues and present thoughtful, researched positions.

03

Monthly mock trials

At the end of every month, delegates step into a simulated courtroom to argue, judge and decide real case scenarios.

04

Critical thinking

Exercises in logic, decision-making and analytical reasoning that build skills useful far beyond the courtroom.

05

Public speaking

Regular opportunities to present, advocate and persuade — in front of peers, in a club that actually listens.

06

Teamwork & leadership

Collaborate on case teams, coordinate arguments across different roles and develop genuine leadership experience.

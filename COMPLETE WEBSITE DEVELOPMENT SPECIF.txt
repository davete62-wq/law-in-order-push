COMPLETE WEBSITE DEVELOPMENT SPECIFICATION
LAW IN ORDER — KB ACADEMY
Build a complete, functional, professional website for Law in Order, a student-led law, justice, critical-thinking, analysis, debate, and decision-making club at KB Academy.
This must be a real working website, not just a visual mockup or a collection of buttons that do nothing. All features must be connected to a functional backend and database. Do not leave fake buttons, nonfunctional pages, placeholder dashboards, or features that only look like they work.
The website should have two main areas:
Public Website / Explore — accessible to visitors and prospective members.
Private Member Portal — accessible to registered users according to their assigned roles and permissions.
The website must work across devices, including laptops, desktop computers, tablets, and mobile phones.

The complete current role structure should therefore support:
President.
Vice President.
Staff Member.
Applicant.

Staff Member
There should be a maximum of five Staff Members.
Staff permissions must be individually configurable.
The President and Vice President should be able to assign or remove specific permissions for each Staff Member.

5. APPLICATION AND ESSAY REVIEW SYSTEM
The application process should work as follows:
A student registers.
The student becomes an Applicant.
The Applicant sees their application dashboard.
The essay topic is communicated individually outside the website.
The Applicant writes their essay.
The Applicant uploads one essay file.
Authorized leaders review the submission.
The Applicant receives a PASS or FAIL result.
A PASS changes the Applicant's status to Delegate.
A FAIL means the application is unsuccessful for that recruitment process.
Essay submission requirements
Accept PDF and DOCX files.
Each Applicant submits only one essay.
No second submission or resubmission.
Essay topics are sent individually outside the website.
The website does not need to distribute essay topics.
Essays must remain private.
Other Applicants and Delegates must not be able to view another Applicant's essay.
Authorized reviewers must be able to open and review submissions.
Review permissions
The President and Vice President should be able to review applications.
Staff Members may review applications if they have been assigned the relevant permission.
The review interface should support:
Viewing Applicant details.
Opening the submitted essay.
Recording a PASS or FAIL result.
Keeping the result associated with the correct Applicant.
The final decision-making authority should remain with the leadership unless the permission policy is explicitly changed.
Recruitment capacity
The proposed maximum is 55 active Delegates.
However:
Applicants must still be allowed to register.
The application system must not stop accepting Applicants merely because 55 Delegate places are occupied.
The capacity applies to active Delegates, not Applicants.
The capacity should be configurable by authorized leaders.

Frontend

JavaScript.
Backend
Python.

Keep frontend, backend, templates, static files, database logic, and configuration organized.
Use environment variables for secrets and deployment configuration.
Do not hardcode production secrets into the source code.

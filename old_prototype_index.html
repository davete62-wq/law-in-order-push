Who we are

More than a debate club.

Law in Order is a student-led club at KB Academy for students interested in law, justice, public speaking and critical thinking.

⚖

Our purpose

To make space for students to learn about justice, examine different perspectives and understand how rules and rights shape society.

✳

Our approach

We learn by doing: debating, discussing real-world issues, researching cases and listening respectfully to opposing views.

↗

Our community

A place to build confidence, teamwork, research skills and the courage to express an informed opinion.
